ABOUT US

The People Behind the Pulses

Two sound engineers, one obsession: turning mathematics into reverb that plays in key.

[PHOTO]

António Pinheiro da Silva

CO-FOUNDER · CREATOR OF THE METHOD

One of the most influential sound engineers in Portuguese music. Trained at Abbey Road in 1982, he has spent more than four decades behind the console — recording António Variações, Rui Veloso, GNR and Camané, and mixing more than 850 concerts for Madredeus and Dulce Pontes around the world. A lifelong researcher in acoustics, music and mathematics, he created the method behind every Trim Pulses impulse.

[PHOTO]

Paulo Lourenço

CO-FOUNDER · SOUND ENGINEER

Sound engineer and musician with 20 years of experience in the studio, on festival stages and in TV broadcast — from records with Sérgio Godinho and Diabo na Cruz to live broadcasts of Rock in Rio, NOS Alive and the UEFA Champions League. He is also the bassist and technical director of the band Criatura. At Trim Pulses he works alongside António and built everything around them: the website, the visuals and the video.
